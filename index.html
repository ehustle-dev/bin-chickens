From Trash to Treasure

Bin Chickens is a WoW Forever guild. We are the ibises of the server: white feathers, black beaks, and a professional interest in whatever everyone else was about to throw out.

The loot system is called the Suptatorship. Superior decides who gets the drop. The weekly tributes further down go straight into his pocket, and paying more buys loot priority in the Supreme Leader's eyes. Trash becomes treasure only if he doesn't want it.

Now accepting tributes. Still rejecting applications.

The People's Main Tank

Superior, guild master and main tank

He holds aggro like he holds a cigar.

Permanently. A bit dangerously. With the calm of a bloke who has never met a threat meter he did not own. Competitor number 112 on the stage, number one on the pull, and the only raider whose pockets get heavier when you pay your dues.

The cap is not a transmog. It is the uniform of the Suptatorship. The banners, the bust, the cigar: that is the whole foreign policy. He will stand in front of the dragon, he will keep the adds, and he will remember your name in the order your tribute clears.

Bring consumes. He likes a raid that has done the reading, and he likes the cigar to stay lit while the rest of you sort out the fire.

How the Suptatorship works (loot rules)

The Suptatorship is the guild loot system. It is short on purpose. Superior decides who gets the gear. A larger weekly tribute buys loot priority in the Supreme Leader's eyes, which means you have been noticed. Noticed is not the same as geared.

  1. Superior gets the loot.
  2. See rule 1.
  3. $50 tier members may be considered.
  4. Need before greed, except Superior needs everything, including the piece you already own a worse version of.
  5. If two rolls tie, Superior rolled higher. He always rolled higher.
  6. The guild bank is a museum. Your tribute is the ticket. The exhibits stay in the case.
  7. Appeals are heard after the lockout. The correct appeal is "thank you, Superior."

Issued from the stage, beside the bust, who also did not win the roll.

Weekly tributes

Three ways to fund the Suptatorship. Call them membership tiers if you want to sound like a shop. They are tributes, they are weekly, and they go straight into Superior's pocket. The $50 tribute is the best value, which is a strange sentence, and also the only one that includes loot priority.

Trash Mob

$10 a week

You may stand in the raid and look at the guild bank. The bank looks back. It does not hand anything over.

  • A raid spot
  • Member privileges in guild chat
  • Guild bank access

Elite

$20 a week

Everything the Trash Mob tribute gets you, plus a seat in the rooms where rule 1 is repeated until it sounds like policy.

  • Everything in the Trash Mob tribute
  • Officer chat
  • Discord privileges

Bin Lord Best value

$50 a week

Everything in the Trash Mob and Elite tributes, plus consumes covered and loot priority under the Suptatorship. This is the tribute Superior can see from the stage. His pocket can see it from anywhere.

  • Everything in the Trash Mob and Elite tributes
  • Consumes covered
  • Loot priority under the Suptatorship

Small print

What's a bin chicken?

The Australian white ibis. It works the bins and somehow looks proud of the shift. So do we, except the bins are last tier's greens and the pride has a raid frame.

Do the Join buttons work?

They do not. Superior rejects every application, which is the fairest clause in the Suptatorship. Try more consumes. No money moves. No form goes anywhere. If a page that looks like this ever asks for a card, close it. It is not us.

Does the $50 tribute get me the loot?

It gets you considered. That is rule 3. Then the raid reads rule 1 out loud. Loot priority is a mood Superior gets when the fifty has cleared, not a receipt you can wave at the corpse.

Is this a Blizzard site?

No. Not affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment. This is a joke. World of Warcraft belongs to them. We just stand in the bad stuff on WoW Forever and call it a guild night.
